## Description

Kopilka turns "I should really start saving" into a plan you can see.

Create a goal — a trip, a laptop, an emergency fund — set the amount and the deadline.
The app works out how much you need to put aside every week and shows how close you are.

GOALS YOU CAN SEE
• A progress ring, icon and color for every goal
• Milestones at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%
• Required weekly pace and a warning when the deadline is slipping

DEPOSITS IN SECONDS
• A dedicated amount keypad with quick values
• "Fill the gap" button adds exactly what is missing
• Withdrawals are tracked too, so the balance is honest
• Optional note for every operation

STATISTICS WITHOUT SPREADSHEETS
• Monthly deposits chart
• Average deposit, number of deposits, completed goals
• Weekly streak of consecutive deposits
• How your savings are split across goals

A PLEASURE TO USE
• Careful, uncluttered interface with light and dark themes
• Confetti and haptics when a goal is reached
• Ten currencies: ₽, $, €, ₸, ₴, Br, ₾, ₺, د.إ, £
• Optional weekly reminder

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No accounts, no sign-up
• No analytics, no ads, no tracking
• Everything is stored locally on the device

Kopilka never connects to your bank and never asks for account access —
you decide what to record and when.
